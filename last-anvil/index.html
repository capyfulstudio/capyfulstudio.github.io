ForgeTaxe: Last Hammer

A push-your-luck forging game for Android, by Capyful.

You are the last smith on the wall. Every strike draws a token from a bag you can see: a spark raises the blade, a crack can shatter it. Push your luck to forge stronger blades, arm your defenders at dusk and hold the wall through twelve siege nights.
