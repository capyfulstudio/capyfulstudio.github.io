Privacy Policy

ForgeTaxe: Last Hammer · Capyful · Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short. ForgeTaxe: Last Hammer is a single-player forging game with no account and no sign-in. Your runs, progress and settings are saved on your device. To keep the game working, understand how runs play, fix crashes and show ads, the game uses services from Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and Google AdMob), as described below.

1. Who we are

ForgeTaxe: Last Hammer ("the game") is published on Google Play by Capyful ("we", "us"). Capyful is the developer name of Ngô Phát Tài, an individual developer based in Viet Nam, who decides how the data described here is used (the "controller" under data protection law). Contact: capyfulstudio@gmail.com.

We do not run servers of our own. The services that receive data from the game are provided by Google, as described below. The game has no account system, and it never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. What stays on your device

These are stored in the game's own storage on your device and are not sent to us:

  • your current run, progress and statistics;
  • your settings (sound, music, haptics, language) and your ad consent choices.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes all of this, and we cannot restore it.

3. What is collected, by whom and why

ServiceDataPurpose
Google Analytics for Firebase App events (for example runs started and finished, nights survived, their results, ads watched), an app instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language, approximate region derived from the IP address. Understanding how runs play, so that balance and features can be improved.
Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations A Firebase installation identifier, country, language, time zone, operating system and app version. Delivering game settings and balance updates without a new version of the app.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports and stack traces, device model, operating system and app version, a crash installation identifier. Finding and fixing crashes.
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) Advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (used for an approximate location), interactions with ads, device and diagnostic information. Showing ads (section 4), measuring them and preventing fraud.
Google Play services Information Google Play needs to install and update the game, and to show the in-app review prompt. Distribution, updates and ratings.

Each of these services processes data under Google's own terms. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Firebase privacy and security page.

4. Ads and your choices

The game shows three kinds of ads via Google AdMob: rewarded ads, only after you tap a button to watch one for a reward; full-screen ads between runs, never during a run and never more than once every few minutes; and a rectangle ad on the run-end screen. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads use personal data, and you can change that choice later under Privacy options in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Purchases

The game sells nothing for real money in this version. If purchases are added later, they will be processed by Google Play; the game never sees your payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

6. Notifications

Any game notifications are scheduled locally on your device and do not use any external server.

7. Who receives data, storage and security

We do not sell your data. Data is sent only to the Google services listed above, over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google LLC and its affiliates (including Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and UK) may process it on servers outside your country under Google's data protection terms. Google keeps it for limited periods; for example, Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for about 90 days.

8. Children

ForgeTaxe: Last Hammer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country). If you believe a child has used the game and want data removed, write to us.

9. Your rights and contact

The game has no accounts, so the data above is tied only to random identifiers of your installation, not to you. You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game, which also removes everything stored on your device; you can reset your Advertising ID in your phone's settings, and Google deletes the remaining data after its retention periods. For questions or requests about this policy, write to capyfulstudio@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

10. This website

This website uses no cookies, no scripts and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive your IP address when you open a page, as with any website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
