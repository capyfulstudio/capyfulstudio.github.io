Privacy Policy

Tugloop: Rope Knot Escape · Capyful · Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short. Tugloop has no account and no sign-in. Your progress is saved on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob; ads may use your device's advertising ID and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. If you choose Accept on the privacy screen, Google Firebase also records how levels are played and sends crash reports; if you choose Not now, it does not. An optional purchase, when offered, is handled by Google Play. You can change your choice at any time under Settings in the game.

1. Who we are

Tugloop ("the game") is published on Google Play by Capyful ("we", "us"). Capyful is the developer name of Ngô Phát Tài, an individual developer based in Viet Nam, who decides how the data described here is used (the "controller" under data protection law). Contact: developercrazygames@gmail.com.

We do not run servers of our own. The services that receive data from the game are provided by Google, as described below. The game has no account system, and it never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

2. What stays on your device

These are stored in the game's own storage on your device and are not sent to us:

  • your level progress and the coins you earn in the game ("coils");
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, board look, analytics on or off);
  • your answer on the privacy screen;
  • a record of purchases you made, so that Remove Ads keeps working offline;
  • your Support ID, a random 8-character code created on your device (see section 9).

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes all of this, and we cannot restore it.

3. The privacy screen and your choices

  • Before you answer. On first launch the game shows a privacy screen with links to this page and to the Terms of Use. Until you answer it, the game reads only what is stored on your device: it does not contact Firebase, Google's ad services or Google Play billing.
  • Accept turns on Firebase Analytics (section 4) and Crashlytics crash reports (section 5).
  • Not now leaves them off: no analytics events and no crash reports are sent, and events are not kept for later.
  • After either answer, the game loads ads (section 7) and asks Firebase for game settings (section 6). Choosing "Not now" does not turn off ads.
  • Ads consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions where Google requires it). A consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether ads may be personalised and whether the ad partners it lists may use data for the purposes it shows. Ads wait for this answer.
  • Changing your mind. In Settings:
    • Analytics: on / off switches analytics and crash reports. When you switch it off, analytics stops at once; crash reporting stops from the next time you open the game.
    • Privacy options, shown where the ads consent form applies, opens that form again so you can change your answers.
    A change applies from that moment on; it does not affect data already sent while your earlier choice was in place.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).

4. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics), only after Accept

The game records these events:

EventWhat it contains
Level started / level endedthe level number, and whether it was won (or lost after running out of hearts and declining a revive)
Coins earnedhow many coins were earned and where from (a win, or a doubled win reward)
Reward video resultwhich offer it was (double reward, revive or hint) and whether the reward was earned
Ad opportunity / ad finishedthe ad format (full-screen or reward video), where in the game it was asked for, whether it was allowed and shown, and how it ended
Ad impressionthe ad revenue reported by AdMob for one ad: network, format, ad unit, value and currency
Purchase offer viewed / purchase resultthe product (Remove Ads) and the result (bought, pending, already owned, cancelled, failed, restored)
Number of purchasesa count, stored as a user property
Firebase's automatic eventsfirst open, session start, engagement time, screen views, app update and in-app purchase (product, price, currency)

Each event also carries the game version, device model, operating system version, language, and a country or region that Google works out from your IP address. Events are linked to an app instance ID that Firebase creates on your device. Analytics in this game does not read your advertising ID or your device's Android ID, and receives no advertising signals. The events contain no name, email address or Support ID.

We use these events only to see which levels are too hard or too easy, where players stop, and how often ads appear, so we can improve the game. We keep analytics data for 14 months. Google signals and the data sharing settings of Google Analytics are turned off for this game, so Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider.

5. Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics), only after Accept

If the game crashes or hits a serious error, a report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics: the technical details of the error (stack trace), the game's state at that moment, the game version, device model, operating system version, free memory and disk space, when the game was in the foreground, and a Crashlytics installation ID. We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days.

6. Game settings from the server (Google Firebase Remote Config)

After you answer the privacy screen, whichever answer you give, the game asks Firebase for updated settings. These settings control whether the Remove Ads purchase is offered, which ad formats are on and how often full-screen ads may appear, and the minimum game version (if your version is too old, the game asks you to update from Google Play). The request carries a Firebase installation ID, the game version, the device's language, time zone, country and operating system version. It carries no gameplay events, and it is used only to deliver these settings.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner at the bottom of the screen and full-screen ads between levels. Full-screen ads appear only when you close the win screen, never before level 10, never twice within two minutes, and not right after you open the game or watch a reward video. Reward videos play only when you tap a button that says what you will get.

To show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, and performance information such as app launch time;
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, video plays).

The consent form (section 3) is also provided by Google and sends similar device information. Where your answers or your device settings do not allow personalised ads, Google shows non-personalised ads, which still use this data for delivery, frequency limits, measurement and fraud prevention. Google uses this data under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Under some US state laws (for example California), giving data to an ad network for personalised advertising can count as "sharing" or "selling". We do not sell data for money. To opt out, reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device settings, or use Settings › Privacy options in the game where it is shown for your region.

8. Purchases (Google Play)

The game may offer an optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads", which stops the banner and the full-screen ads; reward videos stay available. It is not offered in every version or region, and when it is, it appears in Settings together with "Restore purchases". Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms; we never see your card or bank details, and the game does not read your email address. The game receives from Google Play a confirmation of the purchase (product, order identifier, purchase time and purchase token), keeps a record of it on your device, and uses it to unlock and restore the purchase. If you chose Accept, Firebase Analytics records that a purchase took place (section 4). The in-app purchase library in the game sends no data to Unity.

9. Your rights, deletion requests and the Support ID

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy. We will not treat you differently for asking.

  • To ask us: use Contact us in Settings, which opens an email to developercrazygames@gmail.com with your Support ID and game version filled in, or write to that address yourself. We answer within 30 days.
  • Deleting analytics data: if you accepted analytics, the email that Contact us opens also contains your Firebase app instance ID, a random identifier of this installation that Firebase already uses. Send it with your request and we will delete the analytics data linked to it through Google's deletion tools. If you chose "Not now", the email says "Analytics: off" and there is no analytics data to delete. Crash reports carry no such identifier and are deleted automatically after about 90 days; analytics records otherwise expire after 14 months. To stop new records, switch Analytics off in Settings.
  • The Support ID only lets us recognise your messages; it is never sent with analytics or crash reports.
  • Ads data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Google's own controls are described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Data on your device: uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Purchases: records of Google Play purchases are kept by Google; see Google Play's help pages.
  • You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

11. Who receives data, and where

Only Google receives data from the game: Google LLC and its affiliates (including Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and UK) as the provider of Firebase, AdMob, the User Messaging Platform and Google Play. Through AdMob, Google may pass ad requests to the ad partners shown on the consent form. Google may process data in the United States and other countries; transfers are covered by Google's data protection terms. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data for money.

12. Children

Tugloop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country). If you are a parent or guardian and your child plays the game, you can switch Analytics off in Settings, reset or delete the device's advertising ID, and write to us with any question.

13. Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Google. We hold no database of our own. Access to the Firebase and AdMob consoles is limited to us and protected by two-step verification.

14. This website

This website uses no cookies, no scripts and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive your IP address when you open a page, as with any website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

15. Changes to this policy

If a new version of the game, or a change to its server settings, collects anything not described here, we update this page before that change reaches you, change the date at the top, and update the Google Play "Data safety" section to match. If the change needs your consent, the game asks you again.
